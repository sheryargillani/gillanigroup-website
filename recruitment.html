Questions

Recruitment FAQ

How long does it take to bring workers to Romania?

It depends mainly on how long the authorities take to issue the work permit and the long-stay visa. It often takes several months, so we recommend starting early.

Which countries do your candidates come from?

We work with vetted partners in South Asia, including Pakistan, India, Nepal, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka, depending on the roles you need.

Do candidates pay to get a job?

No. We never ask candidates for money to get a job. Our services are paid by the employer.

What does the employer need to provide?

A signed employment contract with a legal salary, the documents for the work permit, and information about accommodation and working conditions.

Can you provide temporary or seasonal workers?

Yes. Tell us the number of workers, the period and the location, and we will propose a solution.
