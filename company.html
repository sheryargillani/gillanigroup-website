Gillani Group S.R.L.

A Romanian limited liability company and the legal entity behind all Gillani Group businesses.

Who we are

An owner-managed group

Gillani Group is a Romanian company built to run a group of focused businesses under one owner: trade and online retail, fashion brands, and workforce recruitment.

Each division serves a different market, but all of them share the same legal entity, the same standards and the same management. When you work with any Gillani Group business, your contract is with Gillani Group S.R.L.
