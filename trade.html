Trade & E-commerce

We supply goods to businesses, connect buyers with suppliers, and sell selected products online to consumers in the European Union.

What we do

Four ways we trade

B2B

Wholesale

Bulk supply of general merchandise to retailers and businesses in Romania and the EU, with repeat orders and invoicing in the company's name.

Agency

Trade intermediation

We find and verify suppliers, negotiate price and terms, and coordinate orders and delivery between buyers and producers.

B2C

Online retail

We sell selected products to consumers through our own online stores and through European online marketplaces, with tracked delivery and EU consumer rights.

Brands

Retail partnerships

We sell on behalf of brands and producers who want to reach customers in Romania and the wider European market.

Questions

Trade FAQ

Do you sell to businesses outside Romania?

Yes. We supply business customers across the European Union and invoice in the name of Gillani Group S.R.L.

Is there a minimum order for wholesale?

It depends on the product and the supplier. Send us your request and we will confirm quantities, prices and delivery times.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. We buy only from authorised wholesalers, brand distributors and producers, and we keep the invoices for every product line.

Can I return an online purchase?

Yes. Consumers in the EU can withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days of delivery, in line with EU consumer law.

How can I become a supplier?

Send us your catalogue, price list and delivery terms through the contact form or by email. We reply to B2B enquiries within two working days.

Suppliers and business buyers

Send us your catalogue or your purchase request. We reply to B2B enquiries within two working days.
