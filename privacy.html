Privacy Policy

GILLANI GROUP S.R.L. · CUI 55657777 · Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains how GILLANI GROUP S.R.L. ("we") processes personal data when you visit this website or contact us, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

GILLANI GROUP S.R.L., CUI 55657777, registered office Str. Florilor, Bl. 1, Sc. 1, Ap. 22, Oraș Bălan, Jud. Harghita, România. Contact: admin@gillanigroup.eu, +40 773 752 078.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: name, company, phone number, email and the content of your message.
  • For candidates: CV details, work experience and documents you choose to share for a job application.
  • For customers and business partners: order, invoicing and delivery details.
  • Technical data our hosting provider records automatically, such as IP address and browser type, for security.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and take steps before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To deliver orders, issue invoices and meet tax and accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).
  • To match candidates with employers, only with the candidate's agreement (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)).
  • To keep the website secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who we share it with

Our hosting provider, couriers and payment providers for orders, employers you apply to through us, and public authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries: up to 2 years. Candidate files: up to 2 years after the last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Accounting documents: as long as Romanian law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. Contact us at admin@gillanigroup.eu. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro).

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
