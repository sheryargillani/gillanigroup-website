Italy opens 165,850 work visas for 2027: dates and how employers can prepare

Decreto Flussi 2027: 165,850 entries for non-EU workers, sector quotas, pre-filling from 23 October to 7 December 2026, click days in January and February 2027, and new rules.

← All articlesPublished 24 October 2026 · 5 min read

Good news from Italy: for 2027 the Italian government has authorised 165,850 entries for workers from outside the EU. The operational circular was published on 1 October 2026, and employers can already prepare their applications. Here is what you need to know.

How many workers, and in which sectors

  • 89,000 seasonal jobs in agriculture and tourism
  • 76,200 non-seasonal jobs, including 14,000 places reserved for family and home care
  • 650 places for self-employed work

Source: QuiFinanza and Finanza.com. The 2027 quota is part of Italy’s three-year plan of almost 500,000 work visas for 2026–2028.

Key dates for employers

  1. 23 October – 7 December 2026: pre-filling of applications on the Ministry of the Interior’s online portal (Stranieri in Italia). Pre-filling is not yet the submission.
  2. 9 – 13 December 2026: finalising the pre-filled applications so they are ready to send.
  3. Click days 2027, when applications are sent, from 9:00 (QuiFinanza):
    • 12 January – seasonal agriculture
    • 9 February – seasonal tourism
    • 16 February – non-seasonal work
    • 18 February – family and home care
  4. Applications can be submitted until 31 December 2027 or until the quotas are used up.

New rules that make the process clearer

  • After the visa checks are complete, the employer has 15 days to confirm the request; otherwise it is rejected.
  • After the worker arrives, the deadline to sign the residence contract and send it to the immigration desk is extended from 8 to 15 days.

Source: QuiFinanza, Fanpage.

Why this matters

Italy, like Romania and Germany, faces serious shortages in agriculture, tourism, construction, transport and care. A predictable calendar and clear quotas let employers plan months ahead. Because quotas are limited and click days are competitive, the employers who prepare early have the best chance.

How to prepare now

  • Decide how many workers you need, for which roles and from when.
  • Pre-fill your applications before 7 December 2026.
  • Prepare clear job offers, salary, working hours and accommodation details.
  • Select candidates early and check that their passports and documents are ready.
