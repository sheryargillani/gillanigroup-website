How we work

From request to first shift

A typical international placement moves through six stages. We keep you informed at each one, and timelines depend mainly on permit and visa processing.

Needs assessment

We agree on roles, number of workers, skills, salary, accommodation and start date.

Sourcing

Our partners in the source country shortlist candidates who match the job profile.

Screening & selection

Trade tests, document checks and interviews, with the employer taking part in the final choice.

Work permit

We help the employer prepare the work permit (aviz de muncă) application for the selected workers.

Visa & travel

Candidates apply for the long-stay work visa and we coordinate travel once it is issued.

Arrival & onboarding

Airport pickup, residence permit steps, workplace introduction and follow-up after placement.
